> This document defines what Factor One builds, how products are selected, developed, launched and improved. Every product decision must align with this document.

---

# 1. Product Philosophy

A Factor One product exists to solve a genuine ownership problem.

Every product should improve the ownership experience through thoughtful engineering, precise fitment and long-term reliability.

We do not build products because they are popular.

We build products because they deserve to exist.

---

# 2. Definition of a Product

A Factor One product is not an accessory.

It is an engineered improvement to vehicle ownership.

Every product should feel like a natural extension of the vehicle rather than an aftermarket addition.

If removing the Factor One logo would make someone assume the product came from the manufacturer, we have succeeded.

---

# 3. Product Principles

Every product should:

- Solve a real ownership problem.
- Integrate naturally with the vehicle.
- Improve functionality or usability.
- Be engineered before it is marketed.
- Be simple to understand and install.
- Deliver long-term value.
- Earn customer trust.

If a product cannot satisfy these principles, it should not be developed.

---

# 4. What We Build

Factor One develops premium automotive accessories that improve ownership.

Initial product categories include:

- Interior
- Exterior
- Protection
- Storage

Additional categories may be introduced only if they remain consistent with our philosophy.

---

# 5. Product Hierarchy

Products are organised using the following hierarchy.

Brand

↓

Vehicle

↓

Category

↓

Product

Every product belongs to one vehicle and one category.

This structure should remain consistent across engineering, manufacturing, inventory, documentation and the website.

Every product follows the same lifecycle.

↓

↓

↓

↓

Manufacturing

↓

Launch

↓

Continuous Improvement

---

# 7. Product Selection Criteria

Before development begins, every proposed product should answer the following questions.

- Does it solve a genuine ownership problem?
- Does it improve the ownership experience?
- Is the improvement meaningful?
- Can we build it better than existing alternatives?
- Will customers recommend it?
- Does it strengthen the Factor One brand?

If the answer to any question is no, the product should be reconsidered.

---

# 8. Engineering Standards

Products should prioritise:

- fitment
- durability
- material quality
- safety
- ease of installation
- ease of maintenance
- manufacturing consistency

Engineering decisions should always outweigh cosmetic preferences.

---

# 9. Launch Standards

A product is not ready for launch until it has:

- completed validation
- verified fitment
- installation instructions
- accurate specifications
- professional photography
- quality packaging
- customer support documentation

Shipping an unfinished product damages trust.

---

# 10. Continuous Improvement

Product development does not end at launch.

Customer feedback, warranty data, installation feedback and manufacturing insights should continuously improve future revisions.

Iteration is expected.

Complacency is not.

---

# 11. Product Retirement

Products should be reviewed periodically.

A product may be redesigned, replaced or retired when:

- a significantly better solution exists
- customer needs change
- engineering standards improve
- vehicle demand declines
- manufacturing quality cannot be maintained

Products should never remain in the catalogue simply because they have always been there.

---

# 12. Product Decision Filter

Before approving any product, ask:

- Does this solve a real problem?
- Does it improve ownership?
- Does it deserve to exist?
- Would we proudly install it on our own vehicle?
- Does it strengthen trust?
- Will we still be proud of this product ten years from now?

If the answer to any question is no, the decision should be reconsidered.

---

# Closing Statement

Every Factor One product should make ownership measurably better.

Our catalogue should never become a collection of accessories.

It should become a collection of thoughtful engineering solutions that customers trust without hesitation.
